Security
🔒
Encryption NaCl box. X25519 key exchange. XSalsa20-Poly1305.
Postbox Sees encrypted bytes. Can't read content. Can't identify senders.
Identity Local keypair. Never leaves your machine. No server accounts.
Delivery Messages auto-delete from postbox after pickup or 7-day expiry.
One command.
Install, set your name, share your code. That's it.
