Security
🔒
encryption
NaCl box. X25519 + XSalsa20-Poly1305. End-to-end.
postbox
Sees encrypted bytes. Can't read content or identify senders.
identity
Local keypair. Never uploaded. No server accounts.
ephemeral
Messages auto-delete from postbox after pickup or 7 days.
One command.
Install. Set your name. Share your code. Done.
