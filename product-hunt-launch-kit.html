4 Full Description OPTIONAL — product page body

Send messages to other Claude Code users without leaving your terminal. You're reviewing code, you spot something, you want to tell someone. Instead of switching to Slack and losing your train of thought, you say: "send Sarah: the retry logic in payments.ts line 84 is going to loop forever, can you look?" She opens Claude Code the next morning. The message is there. Install: claude mcp add answering-machine -- npx answering-machine Tell Claude "set up my answering machine" and it gives you an invite code. Share it with a colleague once. After that you just use their name. Messages are encrypted on your machine before they go anywhere. The postbox (a Cloudflare Worker) holds sealed bytes it can't read and deletes them after pickup. You can self-host the postbox if you want — it's about 50 lines of code.

5 FAQs OPTIONAL

Q: Is this just Slack?
No. You don't leave Claude Code. There are no channels, no threads, no notifications. You send something, it waits until the other person asks for it.
Q: What if both people are offline?
The postbox holds it. It's a Cloudflare Worker, always on. When the recipient opens Claude Code, the message syncs down.
Q: Can the postbox read my messages?
No. Messages are encrypted on your machine before they leave. The postbox holds bytes it can't decrypt.
Q: Does this only work with Claude Code?
Right now, yes. It's an MCP server. If other editors adopt MCP (Cursor, Windsurf, etc.) it would work there too.
